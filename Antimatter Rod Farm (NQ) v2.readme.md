# Antimatter Rod Farm (NQ) v2

Requires 58 GW of Dyson Sphere Power at 99% Ray Receiver Efficiency. 10 of these fit around band of middle of planet.

Inputs: Blue Proliferator, Deuterium, Iron Ore, Sulfur, Titanium Ore, Silicon Ore, Copper Ore, Fire Ice, Warpers.

Produces 450 Antimatter rods a minute. 

The hydrogen > deuterium particle collider isn't proliferated to avoid over producing deuterium. If you want to overproduce and ship it out (albeit VERY slowly), add a sprayer to the hydrogen line entering the particle collider
