# 100 Band

100 Quantum Chemical Plants: 7,500 nanotubes/min, 18,000 stalagmite/min, about 340 proliferator/min (240 for the stalagmite, 100 for spraying the nanotubes).

Band: 100-segment band, 55.8 to 64.8 deg. Fills nearly the full height of the band. In the northern hemisphere rotate 180 deg.

ILS: Spiniform Stalagmite local receive / remote provide, Proliferator Mk.III local + remote receive, Carbon Nanotube local + remote provide, Space Warper local + remote receive.

Quantum Chemical Plants in extra-products mode with Mk.III: 75 nanotubes/min and 180 stalagmite/min each. Nanotubes are sprayed with Mk.III by 3 spray coaters before they enter the ILS.
Tesla towers included; no power generation.
