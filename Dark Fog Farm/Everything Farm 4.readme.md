# Everything Farm 4

This is the 4th in the "Everything Farm" series of BPs. It requires the 300 facility blueprint tech and super magnetic field generator tech due to the way belts are utlilized. The BP will attempt to make organic crystals from the plant fuel and logs drops in order to then make additional titanium crystals for yellow science. If you want to leverage that, you'll need to ship in water via logistics bot. That's impossible to BP given that water for every planet is different. 

Be aware that drops won't show up in the BAB filter until you have researched it. So if you're wondering why you aren't seeing titanium ingots or titanium crystals in the loot pool, make sure you have them researched in the tech tree. 

The PLS do not have ships auto-set so you'll need to manually add them if you want them. Given the low throughput at low VU, I normally skip them here and simply put some in the PLS feeding my science build.
