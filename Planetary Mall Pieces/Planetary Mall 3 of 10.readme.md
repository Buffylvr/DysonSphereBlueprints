# Planetary Mall 3 of 10

NEED TO VALIDATE THE REFINED OIL FACTORY STARTS FROM SCRATCH

Makes from raw and ships out via ILS: Matrix Lab, Self-Evolution Lab, Negentropy Smelter, Re-composing Assembler

Unlike the other BPs of this type, this one liberally uses rare ingredients due to size constraints. Make sure your VU can support it because it stockpiles 10s of 1000s of items in storage boxes and ILS

The single oil refinery running plasma refining is intended to clog after seeding the refined oil factory with initial oil. Let it stay clogged, no harm done.
